About us

Cyber World University

Learn. Hack Ethically. Defend the Digital World.

Our mission

Make practical cybersecurity and technology learning more structured, accessible, and grounded in responsible practice.

Our vision

Help learners develop the judgment and foundational skills to contribute to a safer digital world.

Learning philosophy

Build from clear fundamentals, apply concepts through guided study, and practice only in authorized environments.

Cybersecurity education focus

CWU's planned learning catalog includes security fundamentals, ethical testing, network defense, web security, Linux, digital forensics, security operations, incident response, cloud security, secure coding, and career development.

Every offensive security learning activity is intended for authorized labs and systems the learner owns or has explicit permission to assess.
