Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · This is explanatory interface copy, not legal advice or a production privacy policy.

Information handled in this demo

Course catalogues and dashboard examples use static sample data. Form fields are validated in your browser only. The login, registration, quiz, contact, and admin screens do not send form content to a server or store account credentials.

Theme preference

The theme switch may save a theme preference in your browser's local storage. This preference is not authentication and does not contain a password or sensitive student information.

Future services

Real accounts, learning records, file uploads, certificate verification, analytics, and contact processing are not implemented. A future production service will need an appropriate privacy notice and secure backend controls.

Questions about this demonstration? Use the contact preview; it does not transmit messages.
